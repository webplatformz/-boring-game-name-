# Correction and removal workflow

Status: **operational draft — controller approval and mailbox ownership required before launch**  
Joint controllers: **Lucas Schnüriger and Timo Spring**  
Contact: **bundeshauspack@gmail.com**  
Normal response target: **30 calendar days**

This workflow applies to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and challenges to project-created scores or classifications. It covers parliamentarians, other identifiable people in disclosures, and visitors asking about locally or technically processed data.

## Intake

1. Receive the request only through the published privacy address. Do not ask the requester to publish sensitive information in an issue tracker or social-media post.
2. Record a case ID, receipt date, requester contact, affected profile, request type and deadline.
3. Ask only for identity evidence proportionate to the risk of disclosing or changing the record. Do not retain identity documents when a less intrusive check is sufficient.
4. Acknowledge receipt and explain the expected timetable. If a request is complex or unclear, ask a focused follow-up promptly.

## Triage

- **Official source fact:** compare the published value with the dated source snapshot and the current authoritative source.
- **Matching error:** verify that the source record belongs to the correct person.
- **Project classification:** review the classification rule, source material and any reasonable competing classification.
- **Score or ranking:** reproduce the calculation using the recorded dataset and algorithm versions.
- **Removal or objection:** assess necessity, proportionality, public/editorial interest, harm to the person and whether a less intrusive restriction or correction is sufficient.
- **Urgent risk:** temporarily suppress or clearly mark a disputed field where continued publication could cause material harm and the claim is credible.

## Decision and implementation

1. Correct confirmed source or matching errors and regenerate every dependent field.
2. Correct or remove an unsupported project classification. Clearly label a reasonable but disputed judgement when removal is not appropriate.
3. Recalculate erroneous scores; do not hand-edit the displayed number.
4. Where deletion is refused, document the overriding interest or preservation duty and consider restriction, de-indexing or reduced detail.
5. Notify the requester of the outcome, reasons, affected fields and available escalation routes.
6. Apply the change to the current dataset and prevent the next import from silently restoring it. Preserve only the minimum audit record required by the approved retention schedule.

## Escalation

Escalate to the controller and privacy reviewer when a request concerns sensitive political data, alleges serious reputational harm, challenges the legal justification, or cannot be completed within the normal target. The controller decides the request; counsel advises but does not replace controller accountability.

## Launch dependencies

- Named primary mailbox owner and substitute
- Approved identity-verification standard
- Approved retention period for request correspondence and audit records
- Named decision-maker for disputed or high-risk requests
