# Achievements — draft list

Working list of achievements to implement. Edit freely — add, remove, rename, or change thresholds. Each entry unlocks
once and grants **+1 bonus pack**
plus a toast notification. Flat entries only (no tiered/grouped cards).

Linked only from the Home screen, next to the stats panel (not in the tab bar).

## Collection

- [ ] **First Pull** — Open your first pack
- [ ] **Collector 50** — Own 50 unique members
- [ ] **Collector 100** — Own 100 unique members
- [ ] **Collector All 246** — Own every member
- [ ] **State Council Complete** — Own all 46 State Councillors (SR)
- [ ] **National Council Complete** — Own all 200 National Councillors (NR)
- [ ] **Federal Council Complete** — Own all 7 Federal Councillors (BR)
- [ ] **Rainbow** — Own at least one card of every rarity (common → mythic)
- [ ] **Cantonal Coverage** — Own a member from every canton
- [ ] **Party Party** — Own a member from every party
- [ ] **Bottomless** - Own 1000 cards

## Various / Hidden
These are hidden achievements that are not shown in the UI until they're achieved.

- [ ] **Law Student** — Open all disclaimer and legal pages
- [ ] **Killjoy** — Click on the contact email
- [ ] **Multilingual** — Select each language at least once
- [ ] **Sleepless** — Open a pack at 3am
- [ ] **Copy Room Accident** — Own 26 copies of the same card
- [ ] **Mythic Hunter** — Pull a Mythic-rarity card directly from a pack
